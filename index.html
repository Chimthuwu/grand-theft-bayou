The pitch

one highway. three towns. a lot of hogs.

It's a joke and an experiment — a whole GTA in the swamp, built out of dropped asset packs and held together with tape. Drive anything with keys in it, run from the wildlife, and get out of the parish before sunrise.

01

jack anything

Two dozen cars parked up and down Route 9 — wrecks, a Beetle, a Land Rover, a DeLorean at the pumps. Walk up, press F, floor it. Roadkill counts.

02

the wildlife

Feral Hogs line up and charge. Rednecks and Hoodrats come out of the tree line. Put down twelve of them and the Sheriff finally notices.

03

popeyes everywhere

Because it's Louisiana. Nine of them, plus a 6twelve, a BurgerPiz and a taco stand — buckets patch you up, +28 a pop.

04

the getaway

Scrounge four gas cans, reach the old truck past the Ruston line, and leave the parish. Or get dragged back into the black water. Wasted.

The map

chatham → monroe → ruston

ChathamSpawn point. Trailer park, junkyard, the last few bamboo torches, a "Bienvenue en Louisiane" sign nobody reads.
MonroeThe strip. Popeyes, the 6twelve, BurgerPiz, a taco truck and enough streetlamps to make it feel like somewhere.
RustonCity line. Two rows of shopfronts, an empty lot, and one truck with the keys in it and just enough gas — if you found the cans.
